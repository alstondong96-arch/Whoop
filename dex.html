Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Overview

This personal health tracking application is designed to help its owner review WHOOP health and fitness data.

Information Collected

If authorized, the application may access WHOOP data such as sleep, recovery, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and workouts.

Use of Information

Data is used solely for personal health tracking and analysis.

Data Sharing

Personal health data will not be sold. Any third-party processing or sharing will be disclosed before it occurs.

Security

Reasonable measures will be taken to protect personal data and access credentials.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the contact information provided in the developer dashboard.
